How HiLancer's Hybrid AI Engine Works

Unlike standard TF-IDF recommenders, our system combines 4 intelligent layers for optimal precision.

Layer 1: Sentence Transformer

Encodes job descriptions and user resumes using all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings to compute deep cosine similarity.

Layer 2: Content Skill Match

Evaluates technical skills overlap, domain experience levels, and job categories for exact match coverage.

Layer 3: Trending Signals

Factors in job popularity scores, active applicant volume, client ratings, and recency of job postings.

Layer 4: Weighted Hybrid

Computes Final Score = 0.60×Semantic + 0.20×Skills + 0.10×Trending + 0.10×Recency with explainability.
