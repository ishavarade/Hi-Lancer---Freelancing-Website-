# Hi-Lancer-> Freelancing-Website -> AI-Powered Freelance & Job Portal

An AI-powered career platform built with **Flask**, designed to help freelancers and job seekers discover relevant opportunities, optimize resumes, manage freelance income, and plan their careers. The platform combines intelligent job recommendations, resume analysis, career guidance, and project management into a single application.

## Features

### Find Work Portal

* Modern **2-column job portal** inspired by LinkedIn Jobs and JobFound.
* Scrollable job listings with a sticky job details panel.
* Instant job detail updates without page reloads.
* Displays:

  * Company logo
  * Company name
  * Job title
  * Location
  * Employment type
  * Salary package
  * Experience required
  * Posted date

* Job Information

* Qualification
* Key Responsibilities
* Salary and experience details
